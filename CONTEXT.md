# Domain context

## Taiwan Food Religion War

The game presents familiar Taiwanese food preferences as playful positions to
defend. It does not determine an overall best food or eliminate unrelated
preferences against one another.

### Glossary

- **Food stance（飲食立場）**: one side of a pair of comparable food
  preferences, such as 北部粽派 or 南部粽派.
- **Choice round（飲食抉擇）**: one independent binary choice between two food
  stances from the same topic. A game contains four choice rounds.
- **Stance pool（立場題庫）**: the six approved stance pairs from which four
  distinct choice rounds are selected and shuffled for a game.
- **Belief slate（本局信仰清單）**: the four food stances chosen by the player in
  the current game. No stance on the slate defeats or outranks another.
- **Defense draw（辯護抽籤）**: the non-rerollable random selection of one stance
  from the belief slate for the player to defend.
- **Draw beat（抽籤節拍）**: the explicit sequence after four choice rounds:
  show the complete belief slate, wait for the player to trigger the draw, then
  reveal the drawn stance. It is not skipped or automatic.
- **Drawn stance（抽中立場）**: the one food stance selected by the defense draw.
  It becomes the defense subject but does not outrank the other three choices.
- **Defense challenge（固定質疑）**: a stance-specific, humorous but fair
  opposing question that the player answers in at most 50 characters.
- **Model judgment（模型裁決）**: a personalized judgment generated by an
  installed on-device model from the drawn stance and the player's defense.
- **Fallback judgment（備援裁決）**: the disclosed judgment whose roast did not
  come from the model. It covers both the fully non-model judgment used when no
  usable local model is available or model judgment cannot finish safely, and
  the judgment that keeps a legal model verdict but replaces a malformed roast
  with approved copy. Every fallback judgment is disclosed to the player.
- **App run（本次 App 開啟期間）**: the in-memory application lifetime used to
  scope the once-only missing-model reminder and consecutive-draw avoidance.
- **Missing-model reminder（缺少模型提醒）**: a non-blocking, once-per-app-run
  notice whose primary action continues the game and whose secondary action
  opens the recommended-model area.

### Avoided terms in this game

- Do not call choice rounds semifinals or finals.
- Do not call a selected stance a champion or ultimate food faith.
- `VS` may describe the two comparable stances inside one choice round, but it
  must not imply a cross-topic tournament bracket.
- Player-facing copy must not use a prohibited ranking term even to deny that
  ranking. The result guardrail is exactly `本次抽中；四個選擇都會保留。`
